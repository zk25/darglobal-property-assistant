### Qatar: A Gateway to Prosperity

### Tax Benefits
No personal income tax and no property tax, maximizing returns. Competitive corporate tax rates make Qatar an attractive business hub.

### Permanent Residency
Obtain permanent residency by investing 3,650,000 QAR (1 million USD), providing long-term residency and access to Qatar’s benefits.

### Investor Visa Program
Offers residency to investors who purchase real estate in Qatar with a minimum investment of 728,000 QAR (200,000 USD). Upon confirmation of payment, the Ministry of Justice issues a Sanad Mabdayi, a temporary title deed recognizing ownership. This enables investors to apply for residency even before project completion, under the new government-approved framework.
