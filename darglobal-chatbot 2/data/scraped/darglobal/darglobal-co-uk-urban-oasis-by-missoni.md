## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Urban Oasis by Missoni?
Urban Oasis by Missoni is a completed branded residential tower on the Dubai Canal, developed by DarGlobal. Missoni provided the interior design across the tower's one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments and penthouses, bringing the Italian fashion house's signature aesthetic to a waterfront address in the heart of Dubai.

### What did Missoni design at Urban Oasis?
Missoni's design team was responsible for the interiors throughout Urban Oasis, applying the brand's approach to colour, pattern, and material quality across living spaces, bedrooms, and kitchens. The design concept draws on the energy of Miami Beach living, translated into a canal-facing Dubai address.
