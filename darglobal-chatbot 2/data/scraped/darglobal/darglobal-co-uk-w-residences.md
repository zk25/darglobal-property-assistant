## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is W Residences in Downtown Dubai
W Residences is a branded residential tower in Downtown Dubai, developed by DarGlobal in partnership with W Hotels, part of the Marriott Bonvoy portfolio. The tower brings W Hotels' bold, energetic lifestyle philosophy to a residential address in the heart of Downtown Dubai, with the Burj Khalifa directly outside and Dubai Mall within walking distance.

### Is W Residences sold out?
Yes. W Residences Downtown Dubai is sold out and fully completed. Any available units would be on the secondary market. Contact DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk for guidance on resale availability or to explore current DarGlobal projects.

### What unit types were available at W Residences?
W Residences offered one-, two-, and three-bedroom branded apartments in Downtown Dubai. The tower featured a W Lounge, infinity outdoor pool, clubhouse, cutting-edge fitness centre, a private cinema, co-working spaces and meeting rooms, a game room, complimentary guest suites, and a terrace with a dining area and walk track.

### Where is W Residences located in Dubai?
W Residences is in Downtown Dubai, strategically positioned by Business Bay. Key distances from the entry data: Burj Khalifa 5 minutes, Dubai Fountain 4 minutes, Dubai Canal 3 minutes, Dubai Mall 6 minutes, Dubai Opera 6 minutes. The Burj Khalifa and Dancing Fountains are visible directly from the tower.

### What does W Hotels bring to a residential building?
W Hotels, born from New York City's bold 24/7 culture, brings a "rebel luxury" philosophy to W Residences. Common areas including the W Lounge, lobby, and amenity spaces reflect the brand's energetic approach to design and hospitality, extending the W hotel experience to private residential living.

### What amenities does W Residences offer?
The tower includes an infinity outdoor pool, cutting-edge fitness centre, clubhouse, private cinema and communications space, game room and chill-out area, co-working offices with meeting rooms, terrace with dining and walk track, and complimentary guest suites for residents.

### What are the investment advantages of Dubai real estate?
Dubai offers a 10-year Golden Visa with a 2 million AED investment (now including off-plan properties), 0% income tax, and 0% property tax. It is ranked first globally for safety and is one of the top five most-visited cities in the world, home to more than 120 nationalities.

### Can I buy a resale unit at W Residences?
As W Residences is sold out at the developer level, units are available on the secondary market only. DarGlobal can assist with enquiries or direct buyers to appropriate resale channels. Visit darglobal.co.uk or contact the sales team directly.

### What other DarGlobal projects are available in Dubai?
DarGlobal has delivered and is currently developing multiple branded and non-branded residential projects in Dubai, including properties in Downtown Dubai and Business Bay. Visit darglobal.co.uk to explore the current portfolio and available projects.
