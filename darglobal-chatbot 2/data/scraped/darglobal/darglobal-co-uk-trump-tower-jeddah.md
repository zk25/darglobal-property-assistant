## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Trump Tower Jeddah?
Trump Tower Jeddah is a 47-floor Trump-branded seafront residential tower on the Jeddah Corniche, developed by DarGlobal. It offers one- to four-bedroom apartments and three- and four-bedroom penthouses with direct Red Sea views, alongside the Trump Private Club for residents and members.

### What unit types and sizes are available at Trump Tower Jeddah?
The tower offers one-, two-, three-, and four-bedroom apartments as well as three- and four-bedroom penthouses, ranging from 70 to 365 sqm. All units are positioned on the Jeddah Corniche and face the Red Sea.

### When will Trump Tower Jeddah be completed?
Completion is scheduled for December 2029. The project is under development by DarGlobal. Buyers can contact the sales team directly for current availability, payment plan structure, and brochure download.

### Where exactly is Trump Tower Jeddah located?
The tower sits on the Jeddah Corniche along Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coastline. It offers easy access to the marina, the Formula 1 street circuit, and the King Fahd Fountain, placing residents at the intersection of coastal living and the city's key landmarks.

### Is Trump Tower Jeddah developed by the Trump Organization?
No. Trump Tower Jeddah is owned and developed by DarGlobal Holdings Real Estate, which uses the Trump name and mark under a licensing agreement. The Trump Organization is not the owner, developer, or seller of the property.

### What is the Trump Private Club at Trump Tower Jeddah?
The Trump Private Club is a resident-focused facility within the tower offering fine dining, a cigar lounge, meeting rooms, a gym, a yoga zone, swimming pool pool, kids pool, jacuzzi, and 24/7 concierge services.

### What amenities does Trump Tower Jeddah offer?
Amenities include the Trump Private Club, 24/7 concierge, fine dining, and cigar lounge, meeting rooms, gym, yoga zone, pool, kids' pool, jacuzzi, and uninterrupted Red Sea views from one of the tallest towers in Jeddah Corniche.

### Why invest in Jeddah real estate under Saudi Vision 2030?
Saudi Arabia offers 0% income tax and capital gains tax, a 30-year corporate tax exemption, and premium Saudi residency eligibility for investments of 4 million SAR or more. The SAR is pegged to the US dollar, and Saudi Arabia hosts Expo 2030 and the 2034 FIFA World Cup.

### What makes Trump Tower Jeddah's location on the Corniche valuable?
The Jeddah Corniche is the city's most sought-after coastal address. The tower's seafront position, proximity to the marina and F1 circuit, and its Standing as one of the tallest buildings on the strip place it at the centre of Jeddah's long-term urban and tourism growth corridor.
