## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Les Vagues by Elie Saab?
Les Vagues by Elie Saab is a branded seafront residential complex on Qetaifan Island North, Doha, developed by DarGlobal. Elie Saab provided the interior design for each residence and the building's common areas, bringing the Lebanese fashion house's signature aesthetic of cream, marble, bronze, and premium stone to one-, two-, and three-bedroom apartments.

### What did Elie Saab design at Les Vagues?
Elie Saab was responsible for the full interior design of each residence, as well as the building's lounges and lobby areas, personally selecting all furniture for the common spaces. The design palette centres on cream and grey tones, shiny metal finishes, bronze, marble, and premium stone, reflecting the couture-level craftsmanship the house is known for.

### What unit types and sizes are available at Les Vagues?
Les Vagues offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom residences ranging from 86 to 389 sqm on Qetaifan Island North. Units are served by four personal elevators per building including a service elevator, and sit within a gated seafront complex with an infinity pool and landscaped sea-view courtyard.

### When will Les Vagues by Elie Saab be completed?
Les Vagues is under development with completion scheduled for December 2027. Construction is actively underway on Qetaifan Island North. Contact DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk for current unit availability, pricing, and payment plan terms.

### Where is Les Vagues located?
Les Vagues is on Qetaifan Island North, a 1.3 million sqm man-made island off the coast of Doha, Qatar. The development is 30 minutes from Hamad International Airport, within minutes of Qatar's largest theme park, and sits alongside 7 km of beach shoreline, a vibrant marina, and a signature 18-hole golf course and clubhouse.

### What amenities does Les Vagues offer within the building?
Building amenities include an infinity swimming pool, state-of-the-art fitness centre, four personal elevators per unit including a service elevator, 24-hour reception, luxury retail, covered parking for all units, and a landscaped community courtyard with sea views. Lobby and lounge furniture was designed and personally selected by Elie Saab.

### What lifestyle facilities does Qetaifan Island North offer?
Qetaifan Island North delivers 7 km of beach shoreline, private beaches, a marina, world-class boating facilities, boutique shopping, a water park, a winter garden, a signature 18-hole golf course and clubhouse, and 5-star hotels and amenities, functioning as a fully self-contained luxury destination in Doha.
