## Investor Relations
Listed on the London Stock Exchange since 2023, we’re building on three decades of development know-how. Every opportunity is strategically curated and backed by market insight. Our partners represent forward-thinking luxury brands who share our passion for design: Aston Martin, W Hotels, Missoni, Automobili Lamborghini, to name a few.
From London to Dubai, our portfolio spans 14 cities and 9 countries, offering prime access to the world’s most dynamic markets. We serve our international clientele from hubs in New York, London, Marbella, Athens, Dubai, Jeddah, Riyadh, Muscat, and Doha.

## leadership
As DarGlobal’s CEO, Ziad El Chaar leads the company’s strategic expansion and partnerships. Under his stewardship, DarGlobal was listed on the London Stock Exchange in 2023 and has forged exclusive collaborations with likeminded luxury brands, including Trump Organization, Aston Martin, Automobili Lamborghini, ELIE SAAB, Marriott Residences, Missoni, Mouawad, Fendi, Pagani Automobili, Paramount and W Hotels. El Chaar has pioneered DarGlobal’s diversification into the hospitality sector, while also overseeing innovative development projects across key markets in the UAE, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Spain and UK.
