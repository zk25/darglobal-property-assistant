# Dar Global Opens Sales for Trump Villas Simaisma, Qatar with Unprecedented Interest

## Dar Global Opens Sales for Trump Villas Simaisma, Qatar with Unprecedented Interest
Dar Global, the London-listed luxury international real estate developer, has officially opened sales for its Trump Villas within Trump International Golf Club Simaisma, Qatar, witnessing unprecedented demand and strong investor interest since sales launched on 1 October.
The limited release includes three- and five-bedroom Trump Villas, each offering the privacy of a gated community, along with elevated views of the expansive 18-hole golf course, Simaisma beach and the open sea.
Residences are now available to purchase, with prices starting from QAR 5 million. Works on site are expected to begin in early 2027, with completion targeted for the end of 2030.
Designed to deliver an exclusive coastal lifestyle, the development will be Doha’s first gated golf community, comprising a limited number of villas. It will feature a dedicated community centre for residents, equipped with padel and tennis courts, alongside a members-only golf and beach club, one of a kind worldwide.
Ziad El Chaar, CEO of Dar Global , commented: “The advancement of Trump International Simaisma, Qatar, marks a key milestone in delivering high-value, iconically branded real estate in premier regional growth markets. Beyond establishing a world-class championship golf and coastal destination, this project aligns directly with Qatar’s progressive foreign investment and property-linked residency frameworks, offering international investors long-term stability and 100% freehold ownership. Dar Global continues to provide global investors with a structured gateway to high-yielding, lifestyle-driven assets in the GCC.”
The destination offers buyers a rare opportunity to secure a residence within one of Qatar’s most ambitious new waterfront masterplans at an early stage.
