# Painite Villas in Benahavis | Benahavis Property | DarGlobal

## Painite Villas Design Inspired by Automobili Lamborghini

## SIDE BY SIDE
The Painite Villas are the only villas in the South of Spain with the exclusive Sky Car Lift with technology by Automobili Lamborghini. Located in the hills of Benahavis, overlooking Marbella, the Painite Villas rise out from the hills, offering panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea and the Andalusian landscape. The villas fitted with Automobili Lamborghini interiors have their very own private car elevator. The Sky Car Lift has access to both levels of the villa, seamlessly integrating your supercar into your living space.

## PRIVATE SKY CAR LIFT
The entrance to the Painite Villa is nothing short of breathtaking. As you drive into the villa via the Sky Car Lift, you're enveloped in a protective glass dome that offers a stunning 360-degree view of your supercar. Inside, you're greeted by spacious, light-filled interiors where floor-to-ceiling windows seamlessly blend the indoors with the outdoors. Here, your supercar is the centerpiece, showcased like a piece of art against the magnificent backdrop of the Mediterranean Sea.

## THE VILLA
The villa boasts six luxurious bedrooms, expansive roof terraces, and panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea from every angle. Complementing this are the extensive swimming pool and lush, meticulously designed gardens, making the villa a gateway to unparalleled luxury.

## THE VIEWS
The Painite Villas are masterfully positioned on the hilltops, where careful leveling ensures that each villa enjoys unobstructed, panoramic views. From this elevated vantage point, the villas offer breathtaking vistas that stretch across the horizon, providing an uninterrupted connection to the surrounding landscape. The strategic placement of these villas maximizes the stunning scenery and creates a sense of serenity and exclusivity.

## THE AMENITIES
Each villa features expansive roof terraces that provide an ideal setting for relaxation or entertaining, all while enjoying panoramic views of the Mediterranean Sea from every angle. The outdoor space is thoughtfully designed, offering a perfect spot for a pool if desired, and is surrounded by meticulously landscaped luxury gardens that offer a serene escape. Every aspect of these villas is crafted to create a harmonious blend of luxury, comfort, and natural beauty.

## LOCATION
The Painite Villas are located in Tierra Viva in Marbella, one of the most affluent locations around the world. Home to the most stylish clubs, restaurants and hotels, as well as, shopping areas, exclusive marinas and breathtaking beaches. The villas are precisely nestled in the hills, only minutes away from the shores and in close proximity to 9 golf clubs, numerous Andalusian villages and Puerto Banus.
