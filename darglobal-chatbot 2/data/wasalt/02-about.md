# About Wasalt Real Estate Services

- Name: Wasalt Real Estate Services (also called Wasalt Real Estate Brokerage). Tagline: "Your investment partner".
- A real estate brokerage platform made up of a website and an app, offering one portal for property search, buying, renting, real estate marketing and listing in the residential and commercial sectors.
- Aims: verify every listed property, keep listings at premium quality and make pricing transparent.
- Ownership: a joint venture between Dar Al Arkan (a Saudi real estate developer with over 27 years of experience) and Quara Holding (a technology start-up incubator and innovation hub).
- Registered in Riyadh under Commercial Registration No. 1010635634.

## Services

- Brokerage in the residential and commercial sectors.
- Expert mediation for buying or leasing approved properties; properties are approved only after quality control, verification and market-based pricing.
- Property management and maintenance.
- Financial mediation at auctions.
- Digital tools for property visits and completing transactions.
- Clear, transparent and accurate property information.
- Online solutions for real estate and rental markets inside and outside Saudi Arabia.
- Access to property specialists and several mortgage-provider options.
- Trained property professionals and a sales team offering round-the-clock support at each stage.
