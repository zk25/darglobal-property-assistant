# Wasalt growth and news

## Growth (reported January 2025)

- More than 750,000 registered users and about 100,000 leads per month.
- Revenue expected to grow about fivefold between 2025 and 2028.
- Described as a leader in Saudi PropTech within about four years of launch.
- AI tools: AI-driven listing verification, a real-time valuation tool, personalised property descriptions and social media content, ROI prediction, and lead generation for brokers, developers and buyers.
- A digital auction platform licensed by Infath, Saudi Arabia's Entrustment and Liquidation Centre, designed to make buying and selling property by auction simpler and more secure.
- Market context cited: Saudi Arabia needs about 1.5 million new homes, and homeownership is targeted to rise from 47% to 70% by 2030.

## Leadership
Source: https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/lists/?p=63970

- Ziad El Chaar is Chairman of Wasalt Real Estate Services. He is also CEO of DarGlobal and Vice Chairman of Quara Holding.

## Partnership with DarGlobal (16 April 2025)
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/press/darglobal-partners-with-wasalt-to-advertise-trump-tower-jeddah

- DarGlobal partnered with Wasalt to list Trump Tower Jeddah on Wasalt's website (wasalt.sa) and iOS and Android apps, to reach more investors and property seekers across Saudi Arabia.
- The announcement describes Wasalt as REGA-licensed, known for verified listings, property search tools, and connecting users with brokers and residential projects; its services cover residential sales, masterplans and auctions, with a strong user base in Riyadh, Jeddah and Dammam.
