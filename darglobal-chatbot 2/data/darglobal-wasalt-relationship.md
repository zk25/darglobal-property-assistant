# How are DarGlobal and Wasalt related?

- Both companies belong to the Dar Al Arkan group of Saudi Arabia.
- DarGlobal (DarGlobal PLC) was established as the international arm of Dar Al Arkan Real Estate Development, to develop its assets outside Saudi Arabia. It is listed on the London Stock Exchange and headquartered in Dubai.
- Wasalt is a Saudi digital real estate platform, a joint venture between Dar Al Arkan and Quara Holding.
- Ziad El Chaar is CEO of DarGlobal and Chairman of Wasalt.
- In April 2025 the two companies partnered to list DarGlobal's Trump Tower Jeddah on Wasalt's website and apps.

## Difference between DarGlobal and Wasalt

- DarGlobal is a developer: it builds and sells luxury branded residences, villas, hotels and golf communities in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Spain, the UK and other markets.
- Wasalt is a property marketplace and brokerage platform inside Saudi Arabia: people use it to buy, rent, list and auction residential and commercial properties, find a broker and get home-loan options.
