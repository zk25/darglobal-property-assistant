# About DarGlobal

## Leadership

- Ziad El Chaar is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of DarGlobal.
- His stated aim for customers: the end result DarGlobal wants to deliver is simply happiness.

## Mission and approach

- DarGlobal develops real estate that combines lifestyle and value.
- It works with trusted global partners to build homes, communities and destinations.
- Core principles: integrity in delivery, care in design and purpose in construction.
- Over 31 years of experience in luxury development.

## Global presence

- 9 sales offices.
- Projects in 11 cities.
- Portfolio: residences, vacation retreats, hospitality, and developments with luxury brands including Aston Martin and Lamborghini.
