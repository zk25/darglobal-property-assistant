# AIDA, Muscat, Oman

## Location and masterplan

- Muscat, Oman, in a hilltop position 130 m above sea level overlooking the coastline.
- About 25 minutes from Muscat International Airport; about a 60-minute flight from Dubai.
- Masterplan by DarGlobal in partnership with OMRAN. A gated community described as a "city within a city", combining residences, hotels, golf, nature and leisure.
- Views of the sea, the golf course and the valley (wadi). More than 100 nationalities in the community.

## Golf and hospitality

- Trump International Golf Club Oman: 18-hole championship course; access by membership, subject to application and approval.
- Trump International Hotel (Muscat), Marriott Residences, hotel residences and hotel rooms.
- Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts AIDA.
- Venues and experiences: SHFT Night Club, AIDA Beach, The Hanging Suites, Trump Members Only Club, 5-star dining.

## Residences in AIDA

- Trump Golf Villas: frontline to the Trump golf course; owners receive club membership and a branded golf cart.
- Fairway Villas: frontline to the golf course; owners receive club membership and a branded golf cart.
- Trump Cliff Villas: 3-bedroom cliffside residences next to the Trump Hotel.
- Azure Oceanfront Villas: 5-bedroom ultra-luxury residences with interiors by FENDI Casa.
- Sunrise Haven Luxury Villas: fully furnished, suited to private use or short-term rental.
- Amour Sans Detour (1 and 2): near the sea and the golf course.
- The Great Escape (1 and 2), Coastal Investment Villas, apartments and luxury villas.

## Amenities

Plazas, cafes and restaurants, fitness centre, mountain-bike, hiking and running trails, parks, viewing deck, swimming pools, promenades, community and retail centre, wellness centres, a life-size chess board, children's splash areas, outdoor cinemas and adventure zones, and the residents-only AIDA Residence Club.

## Ownership, financing and residency

- 100% freehold ownership for all nationalities (100% foreign ownership within designated investment zones).
- Mortgage programme for resident and non-resident buyers.
- Structured payment plans linked to construction milestones.
- Short-term rental permitted for approved properties, with optional professional management.
- Lifetime residency visa for investors in eligible ITC projects; eligible property ownership grants lifetime residency, subject to regulations.

## Oman tax benefits

0% personal income tax, 0% capital gains tax, 0% inheritance tax (home-country inheritance laws still apply), and a 25-year corporate income tax exemption in special economic and free zones.

## Other factors cited

Upcoming UAE–Muscat railway; the Saudi–Oman highway cutting travel by 16 hours; Oman ranked 4th globally and 1st in Asia and the Arab world on Numbeo's 2025 Quality of Life Index.
