# The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin

- Developer: DarGlobal (master developer). Interior design brand: Aston Martin.
- Location: Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah (RAK), UAE.
- Status: under development. Completion (handover): December 2028.
- Property types: beach residences and beach villas.
- Unit mix: 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom residences; 3-bedroom beach villas.
- Unit sizes: the page header gives 682 to 12,277 sq ft; the FAQ gives 61 to 265 sqm.
- Amenities: direct beach access, infinity pool, spa, sauna, pool bar, fitness centre, indoor cinema, yoga lawn, multipurpose hall, kids' play area, premium 24/7 security, and private shoreline access from the beach villas.
- Top of the Astera is a separate one-of-one residence at the same development.
- The Astera was one of the main revenue drivers in DarGlobal's HY 2026 results.
- Prices and payment plans: not published; contact sales.
