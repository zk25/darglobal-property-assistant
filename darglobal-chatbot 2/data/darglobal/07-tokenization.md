# DarGlobal tokenization

DarGlobal offers blockchain-based tokenization of luxury real estate.

## How it works

- Physical properties are converted into digital tokens.
- Each token represents a fractional share of a premium DarGlobal property.
- Tokens can be bought, sold or held, and traded through regulated platforms.
- Every transaction is recorded on a secure blockchain.

## Stated benefits

- Access to DarGlobal projects from any location.
- Liquidity, with tokens backed by real-world assets.
- Transparent, blockchain-recorded transactions.
- Fractional ownership of luxury residences without the complexity of traditional property transactions.
