## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin?
The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin is a beachfront residential development on Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah, developed by DarGlobal. Aston Martin's design team applied the brand's precision and craftsmanship to each unit, producing a collection of one- to three-bedroom residences and three-bedroom beach villas directly on the water's edge.

### What unit types and sizes are available at The Astera?
The Astera offers one-, two-, and three-bedroom residences and three-bedroom beachfront villas ranging from 61 to 265 sqm. All units feature Aston Martin-designed interiors and sit on Al Marjan Island with direct beach access.

### What did Aston Martin design at The Astera?
Aston Martin's design team was responsible for the interiors of every residence, applying the brand's approach to precision, material quality, and artisanal craftsmanship to kitchens, bathrooms, and living spaces. The collaboration integrates cutting-edge design with the timeless finishes associated with the Aston Martin brand.

### When will The Astera be completed?
The Astera is under development with handover scheduled for December 2028. DarGlobal is the master developer responsible for delivery on Al Marjan Island. Contact the sales team for current unit availability, pricing, and payment plan terms.

### Where exactly is The Astera located?
The Astera is on Al Marjan Island, a man-made island off the Ras Al Khaimah coastline, 60 minutes from Dubai International Airport. It sits approximately 5 minutes from the upcoming Wynn Resort on Al Marjan Island, the first casino resort in the Middle East.

### What amenities does The Astera offer?
Amenities include direct beach access, an infinity pool, spa, sauna, pool bar, fitness centre, indoor cinema, yoga lawn, multipurpose hall, children's play area, and premium 24/7 security. Beach villas offer private access to the shoreline directly from the residence.

### Is The Astera a good investment in Ras Al Khaimah?
Al Marjan Island is an emerging market where average apartment capital values rose approximately 35% in a single year. Branded residences historically command price premiums of 30 to 200% over comparable unbranded stock, and the Wynn Resort announcement has been directly linked to market price doubling over two years. Buyers should seek independent investment advice.

### What is the UAE Golden Visa benefit for buyers at The Astera?
Buyers investing 2 million AED or more in UAE real estate may apply for a 10-year Golden Visa. The UAE applies 0% income tax and 0% property tax, providing a tax-efficient holding environment for both resident and international buyers.

### How does the Wynn Resort affect Al Marjan Island property values?
The announcement of the Wynn Resort on Al Marjan Island, the first casino resort in the Middle East, has been directly linked to a doubling of market prices over the past two years. The resort will be approximately 5 minutes from The Astera and is expected to drive further tourism and rental demand for beachfront properties on the island.
