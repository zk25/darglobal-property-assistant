## The Pagani Penthouse

## OVERVIEW
Pagani’s essence has taken shape, for the first time in the world, in a residential building in the heart of Dubai. The culmination of the first ever living experience, by the super automaker is the Penthouse. Spanning 14,768 sqft, where every square foot is worthy of a masterpiece.

## LIVING IN THE SPHERE
The expansive residence extending the entire top floor is accented with the most unique feature of the penthouse, the sphere. A sphere completely constructed with geometric glass panes to give you 360 degree views, from the sweeping Dubai Canal to Downtown Dubai.

## ELEVATING THE STANDARDS OF LIVING
Residents and guests arrive at the Pagani Penthouse in its very own personal elevator. After being welcomed inside, advanced domotics are available to respond to their every touch.

## THE LIVING SPACES
A timeless interpretation of automotive art. The living spaces are designed with heritage leather, and luxurious fabrics that are reminiscent to the interiors of a Pagani car.

## THE PERSONAL SPACES
Traces of the Italian supercar are steeped in hushed tones of the bedrooms, and en-suite bathrooms. The rooms are reminiscent of the ultra-modern interiors of the Pagani, serene and victorious.

## LOCATION
The Pagani Penthouse is perched on The DaVinci Tower, which sits at the heart of Dubai’s cultural district, surrounded by prominent landmarks, such as the Dubai Opera, exhibition centres, design houses, art galleries, and affluent neighbourhoods. This gives you the perfect opportunity to explore various shopping, dining, and cultural experiences by the Dubai Canal, Business Bay and Burj Khalifa.
