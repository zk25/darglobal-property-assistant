# Wasalt corporate products

- Wasalt Portal: AI-powered platform listing verified properties, projects, masterplans and online auctions, with enhanced search for home buyers.
- PropGenius AI Suite: generates personalised property descriptions in multiple languages, creates trend-based social media posts, provides real-time property valuations, predicts ROI and generates qualified leads.
- Wasalt Business: a CRM app for brokers to manage tasks, listings, leads and teams in one place; brokers and developers can also advertise properties for sale or rent through it.
- Wasalt Auction: an online real estate auction service.
- Matchmaker Packages.

## Wasalt Auction: how online property auctions work
Source: https://wasalt.sa/en

- Wasalt Auction is Wasalt's online real estate auction service, run in partnership with Infath (the Entrustment and Liquidation Centre).
- Property owners can list a property for auction, and buyers can bid on auctioned properties online through the Wasalt website or app.
- Wasalt is licensed by Infath as a real estate auction platform, and its brokerage also offers financial mediation at auctions.

## Licences

- Licensed by Infath as a real estate auction platform.
