# Wasalt overview

Wasalt is an AI-powered real estate platform in Saudi Arabia covering both residential and commercial properties, available as a website (wasalt.sa / wasalt.com) and iOS and Android apps. It describes itself as the first AI-powered real estate platform in Saudi Arabia. Every listed property is stated to be verified by REGA (the Saudi Real Estate General Authority), and Wasalt lists villas, apartments and other properties across most cities, towns and districts in the Kingdom.

## Ownership

Wasalt is a joint venture of Dar Al Arkan, a leading Saudi real estate developer, and Quara Holding. The parent companies cite more than 27 years of industry experience. DarGlobal is the international arm of Dar Al Arkan, so Wasalt and DarGlobal are part of the same group.

## Services

- Buy: residential and commercial properties for sale.
- Rent: residential and commercial properties for rent, including daily rentals.
- Projects: listings of new developments and masterplans.
- List your property: owners can list properties for sale or rent.
- Auctions: in partnership with Infath (the Entrustment and Liquidation Centre), users can list properties for auction or bid on auctioned properties online.
- Communities: browse communities and plots with prices, and book plots online.
- Matchmaker: a questionnaire matches the user with a broker based on their requirements.
- Home loans: loan partners offer home financing at market rates.
- AI price prediction and return-on-investment (ROI) estimates.

## Property types

- Residential for sale: houses, villas and plots (land).
- Residential for rent: apartments and villas.
- Commercial for sale: shops, office spaces and plots.
- Commercial for rent: shops and office spaces.

## Cities covered

Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Khobar are named on the homepage; coverage extends to most cities, towns and districts of Saudi Arabia, including Makkah, Madinah and Khamis Mushayt in the listing categories.

## Features

- Search filters by property type, bedrooms, budget/price range, location and furnished or unfurnished.
- AI-driven insights, price trends, price prediction and investment-return estimates.
- Interactive maps of districts and cities.
- Multilingual AI-generated content; English and Arabic interface.

## Partners

- PropGenius (propgenius.ai): AI platform powering Wasalt's AI tools.
- Infath (Entrustment and Liquidation Centre): auctions.
