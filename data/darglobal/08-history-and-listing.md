# DarGlobal history, parent company and stock listing

## Origins

- DarGlobal was originally established to house and develop the international (non-Saudi) assets of Dar Al Arkan Real Estate Development PJSC ("DAARE"), a leading real estate developer in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
- The portfolio grew from one project in 2017, when Dar Al Arkan Global Real Estate Development LLC was incorporated in Dubai.
- Dar Al Arkan was established in Riyadh in December 1994. Wasalt, the Saudi digital real estate platform, is a joint venture of Dar Al Arkan and Quara Holding, so DarGlobal and Wasalt belong to the same wider group.
- DarGlobal is headquartered in Dubai, UAE.

## London Stock Exchange listing
Source: https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2266861

- DarGlobal completed a listing on the London Stock Exchange in February 2023 (trading began 28 February 2023), valued at about USD 600 million (SAR 2.25 billion).
- It was the first Saudi company to list on the London Stock Exchange.

## Chief executive
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/about

- Ziad El Chaar is Founder and CEO of DarGlobal. He is also Chairman of Wasalt Real Estate Services and Vice Chairman of Quara Holding.
