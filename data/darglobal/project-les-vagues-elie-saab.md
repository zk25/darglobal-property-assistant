# Les Vagues by Elie Saab

- Developer: DarGlobal. Brand: Elie Saab (interiors and common areas).
- Location: Qetaifan Island North, Doha, Qatar. Seafront branded residential complex.
- Status: under development. Completion: December 2027.
- Unit types: 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom residences.
- Sizes: 84 to 383 sqm (FAQ: 86 to 389 sqm).
- Building amenities: infinity pool, fitness centre, four personal elevators including a service elevator serving all floors, lounges and lobby furnished by Elie Saab, 24-hour reception, luxury retail, parking for all units, landscaped courtyard with sea views.
- Qetaifan Island North: 1.3 million sqm island with 7 km of beach, a marina, an 18-hole golf course and clubhouse, 5-star hotels, and Qatar's largest theme park nearby. Hamad International Airport is about 30 minutes away.
- Prices and payment plans: not published; contact DarGlobal.
