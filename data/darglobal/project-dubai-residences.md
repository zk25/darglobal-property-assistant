# DarGlobal residences in Dubai

## Trump International Hotel & Tower Dubai
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/trump-international-hotel-and-tower-dubai

- Developer: DarGlobal (master developer). Described as the first and only Trump-branded development in the Middle East.
- Location: Sheikh Zayed Road, at the gateway to Downtown Dubai.
- An 80-storey tower of roughly 350 metres, combining a five-star hotel, private residences and a members-only club. Gulf Asia Contracting was appointed in August 2026 for the podium construction.
- Unit types: 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments and 4-bedroom duplex penthouses. Sizes 764 to 7,720 sq ft (FAQ: 109 to 722 sqm).
- Every unit has a view of the Arabian Sea or the Burj Khalifa.
- Resident amenities: described as the world's highest outdoor rooftop infinity pool, gym, owners' lounge and kids' play area. Hotel: fine dining and entertainment.
- Members-only club (annual fee; residents get priority access): signature bar, cigar lounge, golf simulator, spa treatment rooms, meeting rooms, dedicated pool, wellness and business facilities.
- Status: under development. Completion (handover): December 2031.

## DG1
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/dg1

- Developer: DarGlobal. Architect: Gensler.
- Location: Business Bay, Dubai, directly by the Dubai Canal, near The Dubai Mall and Burj Khalifa.
- Residential tower with studios and 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments of 452 to 2,476 sq ft.
- Amenities: gym, infinity pool, landscaped podium, 24-hour reception, sky garden and yoga studio.
- Status: under development (completion date not stated).

## Urban Oasis by Missoni
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/urban-oasis-by-missoni

- Developer: DarGlobal. Interiors: Missoni.
- Location: Business Bay, directly on the Dubai Canal.
- Status: completed, delivered and sold out; any available units are resales on the secondary market, and DarGlobal can advise on availability.
- Unit types: 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments and penthouses (penthouses with private elevators); 323 to 3,828 sq ft.
- 360-degree views of the Dubai Canal and city. Amenities: infinity pool, gym, grand lobby, ground-floor retail, covered parking, 24/7 concierge.
- Drive times: The Dubai Mall and Downtown 5 minutes, Jumeirah Beach 7 minutes, Dubai International Airport and Dubai Marina 17 minutes.

## W Residences Downtown Dubai
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/w-residences

- Developer: DarGlobal. Brand: W Hotels (Marriott Bonvoy portfolio).
- Location: Downtown Dubai, by Business Bay.
- Status: completed, delivered and sold out; remaining units only on the secondary market.
- Unit types: 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom apartments of 701 to 7,627 sq ft.
- Amenities: W Lounge, clubhouse, complimentary guest suites, fitness centre, private cinema, game room, co-working offices and meeting rooms, infinity pool, terrace with dining area and walking track.
- About 3 to 6 minutes from Dubai Canal, Dubai Fountain, Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and Dubai Opera.
