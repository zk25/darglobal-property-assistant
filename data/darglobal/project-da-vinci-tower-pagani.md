# Da Vinci Tower, Interiors by Pagani

- Developer: DarGlobal, in partnership with Pagani Automobili. Described as the world's first Pagani-branded residential development.
- Location: Downtown Dubai, directly on the Dubai Canal, near Marasi Business Bay.
- Status: under development (completion date not stated).
- Building: 19 floors above the Dubai Canal, plus a ground floor and three basement levels.
- Unit types: 2-, 3- and 4-bedroom residences.
- Unit sizes: 800 to 14,768 sq ft (FAQ: 77 to 398 sqm).
- Amenities: rooftop pool overlooking Downtown Dubai, gym, sauna and steam rooms with separate male and female relaxation areas, 24/7 concierge, private access via discreet elevators, smart home control (security, climate, lighting, music, appliances), private outdoor areas, and a 20% discount on the Insignia Royal Card.
- Interiors: premium leather, Italian marble, kinetic chandeliers, carbon-fibre accents and wood panelling.
- Nearby: Dubai Mall and Fountains about 9 minutes; Dubai Marina about 20 minutes.
- Investment benefits: purchases of AED 2 million or more qualify for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa; 0% income tax and 0% property tax.
- The Pagani Penthouse is a separate one-of-one Pagani residence in Dubai.
- Prices and payment plans: not published; contact sales.
