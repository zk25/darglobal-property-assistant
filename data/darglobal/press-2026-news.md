# DarGlobal news and results, 2026

## Half-year results HY 2026 (interim results for the six months to 30 June 2026, published 30 September 2026, unaudited)

- Revenue: USD 258.0 million, up 66% from USD 155.4 million in HY 2025.
- Gross profit: USD 87.7 million, up 85%; gross margin 34% (from 31%).
- EBITDA: USD 46.3 million, up 73%.
- Net profit: USD 30.4 million, up 149% from USD 12.2 million.
- Main revenue drivers: The Astera, Interiors by Aston Martin, and Neptune, Interiors by Mouawad.
- Gross development value (GDV): USD 23.0 billion, nearly double the prior year.
- Cumulative contracted sales: about USD 3.9 billion across 4,380 units (from about USD 3.2 billion at December 2025).
- Net asset value: USD 613.3 million. Total cash USD 847.9 million (free cash USD 231.6 million; restricted escrow USD 616.3 million). Total available liquidity USD 579.3 million.
- April 2026: closed a USD 250 million syndicated term loan facility.
- Markets: GCC (including Saudi Arabia and the UAE), Europe and the UK.
- Management described performance as resilient despite regional uncertainty, with strong buyer demand.

## Trump Villas, Simaisma, Qatar (press release 7 October 2026)
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/press/dar-global-opens-sales-trump-villas-simaisma-qatar-unprecedented-interest

- Sales opened on 1 October 2026 for a limited release of Trump Villas within Trump International Golf Club Simaisma, Doha, Qatar.
- Three- and five-bedroom villas in a gated community with views of the 18-hole golf course, Simaisma beach and the sea.
- Prices start from QAR 5 million.
- Construction expected to begin in early 2027, with completion targeted for the end of 2030.
- 100% freehold ownership.
- Amenities: 18-hole golf course, members-only golf and beach club, community centre with padel and tennis courts. Described as Doha's first gated golf community.

## New Jeddah mixed-use development (press release 16 November 2026)
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/press/dar-global-expands-saudi-arabia-portfolio-with-new-jeddah-development-valued-at-sar-1-billion

- A new mixed-use development in Jeddah with an estimated GDV of about SAR 1 billion (about USD 267 million); land cost about USD 56 million.
- Includes an exclusive co-branded residential concept with an international luxury brand (not yet named).
- Part of a programme to expand the Saudi portfolio by up to SAR 4 billion, which would take the global pipeline to about USD 24 billion.
- Buyers from 61 nationalities already invest in DarGlobal's Saudi portfolio.
- Concept, residences and launch date to be announced later.
