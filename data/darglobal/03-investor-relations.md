# DarGlobal investor relations

## Company description

DarGlobal PLC is an international real estate developer specializing in exclusive luxury developments across the world's most coveted cosmopolitan cities. It caters to global citizens seeking prime investments, second homes and luxury living, and positions its projects as economic catalysts that unlock opportunities for both investors and the countries it enters.

Listed on the London Stock Exchange (since 2023), DarGlobal describes itself as going beyond property development: it aims to drive economic transformation by attracting international clients and foreign direct investment (FDI). It cites three decades of development know-how.

## Where DarGlobal operates

DarGlobal has a presence in 13 international cities across 8 countries (another section of the page states 14 cities in 9 countries). Its portfolio spans the United Kingdom, Spain, Greece, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, the United States and Qatar.

## Hospitality expansion

DarGlobal is expanding into world-class hospitality, developing luxury hotels in Dubai, Muscat, Riyadh and Jeddah.

## Sales and customer service offices

DarGlobal has 9 sales and customer service offices: New York, London, Marbella, Athens, Dubai, Jeddah, Riyadh, Muscat and Doha.

## Brand partners

DarGlobal has partnered with over 11 world-renowned luxury brands: Trump Organization, Aston Martin, Automobili Lamborghini, ELIE SAAB, Marriott Residences, Missoni, Mouawad, Fendi (FENDI Casa), Pagani Automobili, Paramount and W Hotels. These collaborations are presented as investment opportunities in global cities offering both wealth growth and protection.

## Financial highlight

- Total gross development value (GDV): USD 23 billion.
