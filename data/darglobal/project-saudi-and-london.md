# DarGlobal projects in Saudi Arabia

## Neptune, Interiors by Mouawad (Riyadh)
Source: https://darglobal.co.uk/neptune

- Developer: DarGlobal (master developer). Interior design: Mouawad.
- Location: northern Riyadh, near the Expo 2030 Riyadh site.
- Limited-edition collection of branded 5-bedroom villas; built-up area 300 to 448 sqm (FAQ: 300 to 420 sqm).
- Villa features: master bedroom with walk-in closet and en-suite, secondary bedrooms, family kitchen, guest living area, entry foyer and terrace.
- Neighbourhood: schools, community centre, sports hub, restaurants and cafes, parks, entertainment centres, early childhood centres, retail and grocery, playgrounds, mosques and healthcare clinics.
- Status: under development. Completion: December 2027. Neptune was one of the main revenue drivers in HY 2026.
- Prices: available from the sales team.
