# Trump Tower Jeddah

- Location: Jeddah Corniche, on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coastline; near the marina, the Formula 1 street circuit and King Fahd Fountain.
- Developer: DarGlobal Holdings Real Estate, which uses the Trump name and marks under license. The project is not developed or sold by The Trump Organization.
- Property type: seafront residences in a 47-floor tower.
- Unit types: 1-, 2-, 3- and 4-bedroom apartments; 3- and 4-bedroom penthouses.
- Unit sizes: 70 to 365 sqm.
- Completion: December 2029.
- Amenities: Trump Private Club, 24/7 concierge, meeting rooms, cigar lounge, fine dining, swimming pool, kids' pool, jacuzzi, gym and yoga zone.
- Features: uninterrupted sea views, prime Corniche location, architectural landmark.
- Prices and payment plans: not published; contact the DarGlobal sales team.
- Trump Tower Jeddah is also listed on Wasalt (wasalt.sa and the Wasalt app) under a DarGlobal–Wasalt partnership announced in April 2025.
