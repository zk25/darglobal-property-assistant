# Why invest with DarGlobal

DarGlobal develops premium real estate for global citizens seeking elevated living and long-term value: branded residences and resort-style retreats in locations with investment potential. Headline: "Expand Your World. Grow Your Wealth." Stated reasons to invest in property: freedom to live anywhere, tax benefits, long-term stability and new possibilities.

## Markets

Ras Al Khaimah (UAE), Muscat (Oman), Costa del Sol (Spain), Doha (Qatar), London (UK), Dubai (UAE), and Riyadh and Jeddah (Saudi Arabia).

## UAE: 10-year Golden Visa

- A real estate investment of AED 2 million (about USD 545,000), including off-plan property, qualifies for a 10-year UAE Golden Visa.
- 0% income tax and 0% property tax.

## Saudi Arabia: residency through investment

- An investment of SAR 4 million (about USD 1.07 million) secures Saudi residency and its benefits.
- 30-year corporate tax exemption.
- The Saudi riyal is pegged to the US dollar, offering currency stability.
- Investors can open a Saudi bank account while on a visit visa.

## Oman: lifetime residency

- Real estate investment grants lifetime residency to investors.
- Simplified bank account setup for investors.
- 0% personal income tax and 0% import and export duties.

## Qatar: residency and permanent residency

- No personal income tax, no property tax, and competitive corporate tax rates.
- Permanent residency: investment of QAR 3,650,000 (about USD 1 million).
- Investor visa: a minimum real estate purchase of QAR 728,000 (about USD 200,000) grants residency.
- After payment is confirmed, the Ministry of Justice issues a Sanad Mabdayi, a temporary title deed recognizing ownership.
- Investors can apply for residency before project completion under the government-approved framework.
