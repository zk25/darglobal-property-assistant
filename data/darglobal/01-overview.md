# DarGlobal company overview

DarGlobal (also written Dar Global; legal name DarGlobal PLC) is an international luxury real estate developer, investor and asset manager listed on the London Stock Exchange. It develops, invests in and operates high-end real estate, including premium hospitality, commercial, entertainment and golf communities. Its focus is architecturally distinctive, design-led residences in premier global markets. Tagline: "Global Capital. Enduring Places."

## Key figures (homepage, indicative, aligned with disclosures as of Q3 2026)

- Track record: 32 years of development heritage (through parent Dar Al Arkan).
- Gross development value (GDV): USD 23 billion.
- 7 international markets.
- 12+ global brand partners.
- Markets named on the homepage: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Spain, London and beyond.

## Business lines

## Dar Global Capital Partners (DGCP)

Dar Global Capital Partners is DarGlobal's DFSA-regulated investment management subsidiary, headquartered in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai, UAE. It is DarGlobal's asset management platform.

## Brand partners

Aston Martin, Elie Saab, FENDI Casa, Automobili Lamborghini, Marriott (Marriott Residences AIDA Oman), Missoni, Mouawad, Pagani, The Trump Organization, W Residences by Marriott International, and Paramount.

## Featured projects on the homepage

- AIDA, Oman: a premium mixed-use masterplan described as one of the world's largest premium mixed-use developments, with Marriott Residences AIDA Oman.
- Azure Oceanfront Villas, Oman: ultra-luxury oceanfront villas on the AIDA cliffs with interiors by FENDI Casa.
- Trump International Simaisma (Trump Villas), Qatar: villas within Trump International Golf Club Simaisma.
- Trump Mansions, Wadi Safar, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia: residential mansions.
- Trump International Hotel & Tower, Dubai, UAE: hotel and residential tower; podium construction contract awarded to Gulf Asia Contracting.
- Jeddah mixed-use development, Saudi Arabia: estimated value SAR 1 billion (announced 16 November 2026).
- Jeddah private members club, Saudi Arabia.
- The Astera, a beach home designed with Aston Martin interiors.
